# AI Champions Meetup — Pulse Survey

**Purpose:** Check in on cadence, value, and format after several months of biweekly meetups. Anonymous, 5–7 minutes.

*Design note: sections are deliberately separated — cadence, content, and format are three independent variables. Don't let "should we change the frequency" bleed into "is the content good," or you'll misdiagnose the problem.*

---

## Section 1 — Overall Pulse

1. **Overall, how valuable do you find the AI Champions meetup?**
   (1 = Not valuable at all — 5 = Extremely valuable)

2. **How likely are you to recommend this meetup to a colleague who isn't currently attending?**
   (0–10, NPS-style)

3. **In the last month, how often did you attend?**
   - Every session
   - Most sessions (~75%)
   - About half
   - Rarely (~25%)
   - Haven't attended recently

4. *(If "rarely" or "haven't attended")* **What's the main reason for lower attendance?**
   - Scheduling conflict / meeting overload
   - Content isn't relevant to my role
   - Lost momentum / not sure what value it adds
   - Wasn't aware of recent sessions
   - Other: ___

6. **What's the ideal session length?**

   - 60 min (current, if applicable)

7. **Which format works best for you?** *(rank or multi-select)*
   - Live, all attend together
   - Live with recording available after
   - Recording only, watch on-demand

8. **Should the meetup remain generalist (all use cases, all teams) or split into tracks** (e.g., TA/FA/MO/Data Analytics-specific)?

   - Split into smaller track-based sessions
   - Hybrid — occasional joint sessions, mostly split
   - No strong preference

## Notes on running this survey

- **Anonymity matters here.** People are more honest about "this meeting isn't useful anymore" if they're not worried about it being traced back to them, especially in a champions/volunteer context. Strip identifying info in your form settings.
- **Q2 (NPS)** and **Q1 (value rating)** sometimes diverge — someone might rate content 5/5 but still say they wouldn't recommend it due to time cost. That gap is itself a useful signal.
- **Q4 is conditional** — in most form tools you can skip-logic this to only show if Q3 indicates low attendance, which keeps the survey shorter for regular attendees.
- Consider a **re-run cadence** (e.g., every 2–3 months) so you can trend the NPS/value scores over time rather than treating this as a one-off.
